# Moderation controls

## Superadmin

- See the registered member list and the separate accountless guest list.
- Create a seven-day, single-use moderator token for a selected registered email.
- Grant or revoke global Moderator access.
- Ban/unban accounts, revoke account sessions, and delete accounts.
- Kick guests from tournaments, delete one guest, or select and remove guests in bulk.
- Guest access expires after seven days. Unpaired expired guests are deleted;
  guests with pairing history are withdrawn and removed from guest access while
  their standings, crosstable, and pairing history remain intact.
- Revoke a guest's browser access without deleting tournament history.
- Review, revoke, or delete moderator-token records.
- Review the permanent moderation activity log.

## Moderator

- Create official tournaments.
- Open and manage every tournament: registration, check-in, players, rounds,
  pairings, results, standings, exports, and tournament operations.
- Kick/withdraw players inside tournament operations.
- Join or leave a tournament delegation when they want their name shown as
  delegated staff; global access remains available either way.

Moderators cannot ban or delete accounts, grant/revoke Moderator access, manage
the token ledger, change ownership, or change site-wide settings.

## Public visibility

The public library exposes only the staff directory (Superadmin and Moderator
names/roles). Registered member emails, guest entries, token values, and
moderation actions stay in the Superadmin control desk.

Only an authenticated registered account whose email was selected by the
Superadmin can see and redeem its pending moderator invitation.

The Superadmin control desk is separated into Overview, Accounts, Moderators,
Guests, Tokens, and Activity tabs. Tournament detail pages show the actual
organizer and only explicitly delegated staff; a Superadmin or Moderator who
merely has global access is not displayed as the organizer or as delegated
staff. Public staff visibility remains unchanged.
